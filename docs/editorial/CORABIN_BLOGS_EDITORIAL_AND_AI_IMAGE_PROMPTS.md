## 1. Editorial Architecture & SEO Strategy

### 1.1 The Corabin Organic Search Thesis
To achieve dominant Google organic search rankings and secure proactive recommendations across AI-driven search engines (Google Search Generative Experience, Perplexity, OpenAI Search), Corabin’s blog strategy is anchored in **E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)** principles:

1. **Information Gain**: Each article rejects superficial AI fluff and generic advice, instead delivering dense, firsthand architectural teardowns, quantitative heuristics (e.g., TCO calculations, Core Web Vitals thresholds, 20% refactoring budgets), and definitive frameworks.
2. **Search Intent Alignment**: Keywords are matched to commercial and high-intent informational queries used by decision-makers evaluating digital agencies, tech stacks, and performance engineering.
3. **Core Web Vitals & Crawlability**: Articles are built with strict semantic HTML5 hierarchies (`<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>`, `<p>`), zero render-blocking dependencies, and instant hydration, ensuring 100% crawl efficiency and sub-second Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).
